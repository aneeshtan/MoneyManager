Smarter imports and stronger privacy in this update:

• Import review is much faster — unrecognized merchants are grouped and categorized in bulk instead of one by one, and the built-in category dictionary now covers hundreds more common merchants.
• New Privacy Mode hides your amounts with one tap, and the app now blurs itself in the app switcher.
• You can now undo an entire import in one tap.
• New installs jump straight to Import after setup so you can bring your data in right away.
• Fixed a crash that could occur when saving very large imports.
• Fixed a currency display bug on the Trends screen and in notifications.
• Big performance improvements across Dashboard, Calendar, Transactions, and Search.
